首页 / Disasters / Pandemic
人为灾难 Danger: 严重 (4/5)

Pandemic

1 Priority Actions

  1. 1 Isolate immediately — limit contact to your household only
  2. 2 Stock 2-4 weeks of food, water, and medications before shortages hit
  3. 3 Wash hands for 20+ seconds frequently — soap destroys most viruses
  4. 4 Create a sick room in your home for infected household members
  5. 5 Ventilate indoor spaces — open windows when weather permits

Key Dangers

  • Widespread illness overwhelming medical systems
  • Supply chain disruption — food, medicine, and fuel shortages
  • Secondary infections from untreated non-pandemic conditions
  • Social breakdown from prolonged quarantine and resource scarcity
  • Water treatment failure due to lack of chemicals and operators

Shelter

Stay home. Designate one bathroom for sick members. Seal the sick room from the rest of the house — plastic sheeting over doorways, separate ventilation. Clean high-touch surfaces with bleach solution daily.

Water

Municipal water may fail as treatment plant operators fall ill. Fill bathtubs and all containers NOW. Water heaters hold 30-80 gallons. Boil water if treatment is suspected compromised. Bleach: 8 drops per gallon.

Fire

Normal fire risks apply. Power may be intermittent — use fire safely. Candles for light during outages with proper ventilation. Avoid building fires indoors without chimney.

Food

Stockpile non-perishables early — panic buying empties shelves in days. Rice, pasta, canned goods, dried beans, peanut butter, cooking oil. Grow sprouts for fresh vitamins — takes 3-5 days indoors.

Signals & Rescue

  • Coordinate with neighbors using written notes if quarantine is strict
  • Radio communication if power and internet fail
  • Visual signals between houses — bright fabric for "need help"
  • Community bulletin boards at safe distances
  • Phone trees for sharing information and coordinating supply runs

Common Mistakes

  • Going to the hospital for mild symptoms — overwhelm the system
  • Hoarding — buying more than you need deprives others and worsens panic
  • Ignoring mental health — isolation causes depression and poor decisions
  • Stopping medications without medical advice
  • Assuming it will blow over in a week — plan for 4+ weeks of disruption
