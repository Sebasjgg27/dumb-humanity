Accueil / Scénarios de Survie / Mountain Emergency
High Altitude / Alpine Danger: Sévère (4/5)

Mountain Emergency

1 Actions Prioritaires

  1. 1 Descend below the tree line if possible — altitude sickness above 2500m is deadly
  2. 2 Find shelter from wind and precipitation — exposure kills faster at altitude
  3. 3 Start a fire if below tree line — above tree line use stove fuel or dead alpine plants
  4. 4 Stay hydrated — dehydration accelerates altitude sickness
  5. 5 Do NOT continue ascending if experiencing headache, nausea, or confusion

Dangers Clés

  • Altitude sickness (AMS) — headache, nausea, confusion, can progress to death
  • Hypothermia — temperatures drop 6C per 1000m of elevation
  • Falls on steep or icy terrain
  • Lightning — highest point in the area attracts strikes
  • Rockfall and avalanches

Abri

Below tree line: snow trench or lean-to against a rock face with windbreak. Above tree line: build wind shelter from stacked rocks and branches. Use emergency bivy or space blanket to retain body heat. Insulate from the ground with branches, pine boughs, or snow.

Eau

Melt snow using body heat in a water bottle placed inside clothing, or melt over a small fire. Running streams at lower elevations are generally safe but should still be purified. Dehydration at altitude is extremely dangerous — drink at least 4 liters per day.

Feu

Below tree line: deadfall, birch bark, pine resin-soaked wood. Above tree line: very difficult — use stove fuel, or collect dry alpine grass and moss. Start fire in a sheltered spot. Rocks around fire retain heat for hours.

Nourriture

Pine needle tea (vitamin C), insects under rocks, small game near treeline. Lichens are edible after boiling. The body burns more calories at altitude due to cold and exertion. Prioritize staying warm over finding food.

Signaux et Sauvetage

  • Mirror flash visible for miles in clear mountain air
  • Bright clothing on ridgelines or open slopes
  • Three fires in triangle pattern
  • Whistle blasts — sound echoes in valleys
  • Stamp SOS in snow from elevated position

Erreurs Courantes

  • Continuing to ascend with altitude sickness symptoms
  • Descending in the dark or in a storm
  • Removing clothing to avoid sweating, then stopping — rapid cooling kills
  • Drinking from streams without purification at lower elevations
  • Ignoring early signs of frostbite on fingers, toes, nose, and ears
