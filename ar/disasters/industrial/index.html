بشرية الصنع Danger: شديد (4/5)

Industrial Accident

1 Priority Actions

  1. 1 Evacuate upwind and uphill — chemicals settle in low areas
  2. 2 Cover nose and mouth with cloth — wet cloth filters some particulates
  3. 3 Do not touch spilled chemicals with bare skin — use cloth or plastic
  4. 4 If gas leak, shut off gas mains if safe to do so
  5. 5 Do not re-enter the area until authorities declare it safe

Key Dangers

  • Toxic gas clouds — chlorine, ammonia, hydrogen sulfide
  • Chemical burns from liquid spills — acid and caustic materials
  • Explosions from chemical reactions or pressure vessel failure
  • Contaminated soil and water supply
  • Long-term health effects from chemical exposure

Shelter

Move upwind at least 1 km from the site. If indoors, seal all windows and doors with tape and plastic. Turn off HVAC systems. Move to an interior room above ground level — heavy gases sink.

Water

Do NOT drink from any water source near the industrial site. Chemical runoff contaminates groundwater. Collect rainwater upwind only. Seal water containers if chemical cloud is approaching.

Fire

Chemical fires require specific extinguishing agents — water may worsen some. Use fire only if confirmed safe from chemical contamination. Avoid breathing smoke from burning chemicals — highly toxic.

Food

Discard any food exposed to chemical cloud or spill. Sealed indoor food is likely safe if building was not breached. Wash hands and food preparation surfaces before cooking.

Signals & Rescue

  • Bright fabric on high ground upwind
  • Mirror flash toward aircraft from safe distance
  • Whistle blasts if trapped or injured
  • Radio communication if available
  • Stay visible in open areas for rescue

Common Mistakes

  • Running downwind of the chemical — moves you into the cloud
  • Using water on chemical fires without knowing the chemical
  • Returning too early to assess property damage
  • Ignoring minor exposure symptoms — they can worsen hours later
  • Assuming rain will wash away all contamination
