# Water Wheel & Mechanical Power

The water wheel is humanity's first engine that *isn't muscle*. A river turns a wheel,
the wheel turns an axle, gears convert the spin into grinding, sawing, pumping, and
later — driving generators. It's the bridge between animal strength and the steam
engine, and it keeps working for centuries with minimal maintenance.

## Types

| Type      | How it works                      | Best use            |
| --------- | --------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Undershot | Flat paddles hit by flowing water below | Low head, fast streams |
| Overshot  | Water pours into buckets on top, gravity turns wheel | Most efficient (up to 85%), needs a head of water |
| Breastshot | Water hits mid-height            | Middle ground        |
| Turbine   | Enclosed, water jets through blades | A3+ high efficiency |

Overshot is the A2 workhorse — build a millrace to deliver water to the top.

## From rotation to work

- **Gears**: wooden then iron gears (see [lathe](/dumb-humanity/tech/lathe) for the
  teeth) convert the slow, strong wheel turn into fast spindle spins (grinding) or
  slow heavy pulls (hammers, pumps).
- **Belt drives**: leather/cloth belts transmit power across a workshop — one water
  wheel can run *many* machines (saw, hammer, grinder, pump).
- **The line shaft**: a long axle runs through the workshop, machines belt off it.
  This is the "factory" pattern — centralized power, distributed work.

## What it drives at A2

- **Grain mills** — grinding flour without muscle (a civilization diet booster).
- **Trip hammers** — forging iron and steel (see [steel](/dumb-humanity/tech/steel_making)).
- **Pumps** — dewatering [mines](/dumb-humanity/tech/mining).
- **Paper and textile machinery** — [paper](/dumb-humanity/tech/paper), cloth.
- **Generators** — the [electricity](/dumb-humanity/tech/electricity_generator) node.

## Siting and building

- Millrace (a channel) with a controllable sluice gate for the water head.
- The wheel's axle runs on bearings; keep them greased (tallow/lanolin).
- Winter: ice jams the wheel — build a shelter and a warm-water bypass.
- A failed dam upstream is a flood — inspect upstream regularly.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Can you build a water wheel that delivers usable shaft power?
- [ ] Can you convert its rotation to grinding or hammering?
- [ ] Can you drive multiple machines from one wheel?
- [ ] Does it operate through a full season without major failure?
