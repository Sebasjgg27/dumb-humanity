# Animal Domestication

Animals convert calories people can't eat (grass, scraps, waste grain) into calories
people can — and into wool, leather, milk, eggs, manure, and eventually *draft power*.
The draft animal is the A1-to-A2 bridge: it multiplies one farmer's muscle by ten.

## Which animals, what they give

| Animal  | Products            | A-level of real value |
| ------- | ------------------- | --------------------- |
| Chicken | Eggs (fast), meat   | A1 — eggs are daily protein |
| Pig     | Meat, fat, manure   | A1                     |
| Goat/Sheep | Milk, meat, wool, leather | A1 |
| Cattle  | Milk, meat, leather | A2 (plow/draft power)  |
| Horse/Donkey/Ox | Draft power, transport | A2 |

## The quiet rules of livestock

- **Manure is fertilizer, not waste** — the link that keeps farm soil fertile without
  synthetic chemicals.
- **Breeding**: keep the best-natured, most productive animals for breeding — this
  is how domestication compounds over generations. Never eat your breeding stock.
- **Rotate pastures** or their parasites and diseases build up.
- Fence *before* acquiring animals, not after.

## Draft power (A2)

A single ox or horse can plow in a day what a person does in a week. The [plow](
/dumb-humanity/tech/agriculture) upgrade and the [railway](/dumb-humanity/tech/railway)
both start with animal traction. Draft animals are the muscle that industrializes
agriculture before engines exist.
