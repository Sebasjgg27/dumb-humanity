# Herbal Medicine & Native Knowledge

Herbal medicine is A1's stopgap — useful, but with hard limits. Its real value is
bridging the gap until A2/A4 chemistry produces reliable drugs. Treat herbal practice
as *knowledge*, verify every claim, and never gamble a life on an unverified herb
when a scavenged antibiotic exists.

## The honest framework

- **What works**: willow bark (salicin → aspirin precursor), honey (wound dressing),
  mint/ginger (digestion), chamomile (mild calm), echinacea (weak immunity support),
  yarrow + plantain (poultice), pine needle tea (vitamin C).
- **What does NOT**: most "immune boosters," miracle cures, unverified local legend.
- **Rule**: the dose and identification matter more than the plant. Misidentification
  kills — a known cure is worthless against an unknown poison.

## Plant identification (non-negotiable)

1. **Three-way verification**: two independent sources + your own observation.
2. Learn the local toxic look-alikes first (hemlock vs. carrot, death caps).
3. Small test dose, wait, check. Always.
4. Never gather near roads/industry (heavy metal contamination).
5. Press and dry specimens for later identification — a plant pressed today is
   knowledge for a generation.

## The priority order of medicine

1. **Prevention** (soap, sanitation, clean water) — the real cure.
2. **Scavenged modern medicine** (rationed, stored correctly) — far more effective.
3. **Proven herbal remedies** — bridge, not foundation.
4. Never trade a working modern drug for an unproven herb.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Can you correctly identify 10 local medicinal plants, with 3x verification?
- [ ] Have you tested the dose and preparation of each?
- [ ] Do you treat scavenged modern medicine as a finite, rationed resource?
- [ ] Do you have a documented, growing herbal field guide?
