# Nitric Acid

Sulfuric acid's first daughter. Nitric acid is the acid that makes nitrogen compounds
available — the gateway to **fertilizers** (feed the soil) and **explosives** (feed
construction and mining). The fertilizer is the civilization-maker: nitrogen is the
plant nutrient modern agriculture runs on.

## Making it (the retort method)

1. Mix **saltpeter** (potassium or sodium nitrate — see [saltpeter](/dumb-humanity/tech/saltpeter)) with concentrated **sulfuric acid**.
2. Heat gently in a glass retort. Nitric acid distills off as fumes/vapor.
3. Condense into a cool receiver. You now hold strong nitric acid.
4. Residual potassium sulfate stays behind in the retort (that's usable fertilizer too).

## Why it matters more than most people realize

- **Nitrogen is the world's limiting nutrient for crops.** Before industrial ammonia
  synthesis, humanity's food ceiling was set by natural nitrogen (manure, legumes,
  and saltpeter from guano/caves).
- Nitric acid + ammonia → ammonium nitrate: the fertilizer that made modern population
  possible.
- Nitric acid + glycerine or cellulose → **nitroglycerin / guncotton** — the high
  explosives that blast tunnels, mines, and railways.
- [Gunpowder](/dumb-humanity/tech/gunpowder) also needs saltpeter directly.

## Handling with care

- Concentrated nitric acid fumes are toxic; make it outdoors.
- It stains skin yellow (nitrated proteins) — that's a burn, treat it as one.
- Same glass/ceramic-only containment rules as sulfuric acid.
- **Never** mix with organic matter carelessly — it can ignite.
