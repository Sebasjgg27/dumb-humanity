# The Lathe: Mother of Machine Tools

The lathe is the machine that makes machines. Spin a piece of material against a
cutting tool and you get *true roundness* — cylinders, axles, bores, threads, bearings.
Every other machine tool (milling, drilling, grinding) is the lathe's principle
extended. Master the lathe and you can build the rest.

## The machine in one paragraph

Workpiece held and spun between centers (or in a chuck). A hardened steel tool bit,
clamped to a carriage, is fed along the length. The result: a perfectly cylindrical,
round part cut to exact dimension. Precision comes from the [metrology](../metrology/) standards you cut it to.

## Building one from scratch (the "maker's lathe")

1. **Bed**: a rigid, flat, heavy bar — iron casting or steel. Rigidity is everything;
   a flexible bed means vibration and garbage cuts.
2. **Headstock**: bearings (babbitt or bronze) holding the spindle true.
3. **Treadle drive**: a foot treadle + flywheel turning the spindle through a belt
   — the classic pre-electric lathe (see [water_wheel](../water_wheel/)
   for power upgrade).
4. **Tool rest / carriage**: a tool-holding slide that moves *precisely* along the bed.
5. **Steel tool bits**: ground from [steel](../steel_making/) — a
   sharp, correctly angled bit cuts; a dull one pushes and burns.

## The first parts it must make

- **Threads**: a screw-cutting gear train → bolts, nuts, screws. Interchangeable
  fasteners change construction forever.
- **Axles and shafts**: for wheels, water wheels, and engines.
- **Bearings and bushings**: the moving parts that reduce friction.
- **Bores**: hollow cylinders — the engine cylinder, pump housings.

## From lathe → machine tools

The lathe produces the parts for the **milling machine and drill press** — the 
[machine_tools](../lathe/) family that then makes everything
interchangeable and mass-produced. This is the line that separates village industry
from industrial revolution.

## Safety

- No gloves near spinning work; tie back hair and clothing.
- Sharp steel at speed — loose metal, tooling, and chips are projectiles.
- Clamp the work securely; a part pulled out of a spinning chuck is a missile.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Can you turn a true cylinder to within 0.1mm of your standard?
- [ ] Can you cut threads with repeatable pitch?
- [ ] Can you bore a cylinder that seals (for steam/pumps)?
- [ ] Is your lathe rigid enough for repeated precision work?
