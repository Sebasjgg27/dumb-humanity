# Magnets & Electromagnetism

Magnetism is the invisible hand that makes electricity possible — a generator is
literally "spin wire in a magnetic field." Natural magnets (lodestone) were known
anciently; the *electromagnet* (iron + wound coil + current) is the A2 workhorse
that makes generators and motors strong enough to be useful.

## Natural and made magnets

- **Lodestone** (magnetite): a natural permanent magnet — the ancient compass stone.
- **Strike magnetism**: stroke a steel needle repeatedly in one direction with a
  magnet → permanent magnetization (the compass needle).
- **Electromagnet**: a coil of copper wire around an iron core. Current on → strong
  magnet. Current off → not a magnet. This is the controllable force field of A2.

## The compass (navigation unlocks trade)

A magnetized needle floated on a cork or pivoted on a pin points north. From compass
comes navigation, maps, and long-distance trade — the A2/A3 logistics spine.
(This is one of the highest-value-per-gram devices on the ladder.)

## Electromagnet → the generator's heart

1. Wind a coil of [drawn wire](../wire_drawing/) on an iron core.
2. Iron concentrates the magnetic field thousands of times (relative permeability).
3. Self-excited generators: a small permanent magnet "flashes" the coil, establishing
   an electromagnetic field that then feeds itself — bootstrapping current.
4. The same physics reversed = the **electric motor**.

## Applications ladder
