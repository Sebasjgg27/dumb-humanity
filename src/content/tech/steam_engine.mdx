# The Steam Engine

The steam engine is the first machine that turns **heat into motion**, independent of
rivers, wind, or muscles. It's the summit of A2: everything below (iron, steel,
metrology, the lathe, the water wheel) exists to make this possible. It takes the 
community from "local power" to "power anywhere fuel can travel."

## The concept

1. Burn fuel → heat water in a **boiler** → pressurized steam.
2. Steam pushes a **piston** in a cylinder.
3. A **valve system** alternates the steam between sides of the piston.
4. The piston rod drives a **flywheel** → continuous rotary motion.

## The critical subsystems (each one a discipline)

| System | Failure mode                    | Required skill                    |
| ------ | ------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Boiler | **Explosion** (steam pressure)  | Riveted iron plate, pressure tests |
| Cylinder + piston | Steam leaks, seizes | Precision [lathe](../lathe/) bore, packing |
| Valves  | Wasted steam, no timing        | Mechanical timing (cams, eccentrics) |
| Lubrication | Seized parts                  | Oil from animal fat or [oil refining]... later |
| Safety valve | Boiler explosion              | A weighted valve that *must* release |

## The safety valve is non-negotiable

A boiler explosion kills everyone nearby. Rules:

- Never run a boiler without a functioning, testable safety valve.
- Hydro-test the boiler with *cold water* pressure before ever firing it.
- Watch for thinning, rust, or cracked rivets. Metal fatigue is invisible.
- A full A2-era steam engine is weeks of work — treat each test firing as a bomb drill.

## What steam unlocks

- **[Railway](../railway/)**: steam locomotive = bulk transport of
  ore, fuel, food, and people.
- **Pumping mines**: steam pumps dewater the deep shafts that supply coal and iron.
- **Factories**: steam engines (stationary) drive line shafts like the water wheel —
  but anywhere, with a fuel supply.
- **[Electricity](../electricity_generator/)**: spinning a generator.
