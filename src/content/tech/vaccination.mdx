# Vaccination (First Principles)

Vaccination is training for the immune system: expose the body to a **weakened or
related** form of a disease, and it learns to fight the real thing before the real
thing arrives. It is one of the most powerful ideas in medicine — and one of the most
dangerous to improvise. Read [sanitation](/dumb-humanity/tech/sanitation) first: hygiene
prevents more disease than any vaccine ever will.

## The founding example: cowpox against smallpox

In 1796 Edward Jenner showed that milkmaids who caught **cowpox** (a mild, related
pox) never got **smallpox** (a horrific killer). He deliberately gave a boy cowpox,
then exposed him to smallpox — the boy stayed healthy. The method:

1. Take matter from a **cowpox blister** on a healthy person or animal.
2. Transfer it into a small scratch on the new person's arm.
3. They develop a mild local reaction and (usually) lifelong protection against
   smallpox.

This is reproducible at low tech — if you can find cowpox, and if you understand the
risk of passing on other infections. **Never** source from a person with open sores,
fever, or illness.

## What this teaches us generally

- **Cross-protection works**: a related, milder virus can protect against its deadly
  cousin.
- **Dead or weakened germs protect**: heat-killed or attenuated organisms train the
  immune system without causing disease.
- **Timing matters**: immunity takes weeks to build; vaccines protect *before*
  exposure, not after.

## The honest limits (read carefully)

At A3, most modern vaccines are **not** reproducible:

- **mRNA and most viral vaccines** require biotechnology far up the ladder — do not
  attempt them.
- **Live vaccines** (smallpox via cowpox, and a few others) are the only realistic
  low-tech option, and they carry real risk of transmitting unintended infections.
- If you cannot guarantee sterile technique and a clean, trusted donor, **do not
  vaccinate** — a bad vaccine kills more people than the disease might.

## The real A3 disease defense, in order

1. **Clean water** — more lives saved than all medicine combined.
2. **Sanitation and waste separation** — keep human waste out of water and food.
3. **Quarantine** — isolate the sick, track contacts, and wait out incubation.
4. **Nutrition and rest** — the immune system's raw materials.
5. **Vaccination** — the last layer, used only when it can be done safely.

## Safety

- Sterilize everything: boil needles, glass, and hands for 20 minutes.
- One donor, one dose, one person — never a shared needle.
- Keep strict records: who, what, from whom, when.
- When in doubt, quarantine instead of vaccinate.
