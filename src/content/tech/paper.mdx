# Paper Making

Writing is civilization's external memory, and paper is its medium. When scavenged
notebooks run out, a community that can make paper keeps its knowledge compounding.
The [printing](/dumb-humanity/tech/printing) press makes paper the engine of mass
education.

## The process (from scrap to sheet)

1. **Fiber**: old cloth (linen/cotton), bark, or grass — torn into pulp.
2. **Maceration**: soak + beat into a slurry (a water wheel hammers it faster).
3. **Vat**: dilute pulp in a large vat of water.
4. **Mold & deckle**: a screen (fine cloth or woven mesh) on a frame — dip, lift,
   the sheet forms on the screen as water drains.
5. **Couching**: flip the sheet onto felt, press, peel the mold off.
6. **Drying & sizing**: press flat, dry, and size with starch/gelatin so ink doesn't
   bleed.

## Quality rules

- Even pulp = even sheets. Beat thoroughly; clumps make weak paper.
- Old rag cloth makes the strongest paper; fresh grass makes coarse paper.
- Recycled scavenged paper, re-pulped, is a perfectly good source.

## What paper unlocks

- **Books & records** — the community memory (see [community governance](
  /dumb-humanity/tech/community_governance)).
- **[Printing](/dumb-humanity/tech/printing)** — copies of knowledge for everyone.
- Maps, calendars, seed inventories, and legal records.
- The paper trail that lets a civilization *scale*.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Can you produce a sheet of paper that takes ink without bleeding?
- [ ] Can you make it consistently, sheet after sheet?
- [ ] Do you have a sustainable fiber source (recycled cloth)?
- [ ] Can you size it properly for writing?
