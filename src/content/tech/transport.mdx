# Transport & Logistics

Industry is worthless if its products can't move. A mine, a forge, and a farm in
different valleys only become a *civilization* when something carries ore to the forge,
iron to the farm, and food back to the mine. Transport is the circulation system; this
guide is about making goods (not just people) flow.

## The transport pyramid (in order)

**1. Foot and pack** — always works. A pack animal (see
[animal_domestication](../animal_domestication/)) moves ~100 kg;
sledges and travois drag heavy loads. The baseline for everything.

**2. Cartage on roads** — one horse or ox pulls a cart carrying 10× its pack load.
Roads make carts practical; carts make roads necessary. See
[infrastructure_roads](../infrastructure_roads/).

**3. Railways** — the great leap. A steel wheel on a steel rail cuts friction ~10×: one
locomotive (see [steam_engine](../steam_engine/)) pulls hundreds of tons
on the [railway](../railway/) you already know how to build. Bulk goods
(coal, ore, grain) only become cheap when they travel by rail.

**4. Water** — rivers, canals, and coast are free highways. A barge carries hundreds of
tons with a tiny crew and no rails. Any settlement on navigable water gets cheap
transport for free; canals extend it inland.

## The logistics rules (the real content)

Moving goods is physics; moving the *right* goods at the *right* time is logistics:

- **Warehouses**: buffer between production and demand. Store at the *junction*, not
  in town — transshipment points are where value is created.
- **Standard loads**: agree on cart/barge/wagon sizes so cargo and pack match the
  vehicle; standardized pallets and containers make handling mechanical.
- **Route records**: map the network, grade the roads, and log transit times (see
  [infrastructure_scheduling](../infrastructure_scheduling/)).
- **Priorities**: food and medicine first, fuel second, materials third, luxury last.
  Written priority rules prevent the strongest from hogging the carts.
- **Maintenance**: a wagon is worthless with a broken wheel. Keep spare parts and
  repair shops at the depots (see [machine_tools](../machine_tools/)).

## What to salvage first

- **Wheels and axles** — steel axles and bearings are hard to make; salvage and re-fit.
- **Harnesses and horse gear** — well-made harnesses double a draft animal's pull.
- **Bicycles** — the finest human-powered transport ever made; an entire fleet can be
  kept running with a spanner and air. Bikes outperform every pack animal per gram of
  fuel (none) and per kilometer.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Can you move goods by pack, cart, and rail where available?
- [ ] Do you have warehouses at transport junctions?
- [ ] Are there priority rules for scarce transport?
- [ ] Do you keep wheels, axles, and harnesses in repair?
