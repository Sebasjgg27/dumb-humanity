The rules of the tree

  • You can't skip the ladder. A steam engine without iron smelting is a rumor. Each node's prerequisites are listed on its guide.
  • Scavenge before you build. The modern world left a warehouse of salvaged parts. Always check the salvage list before manufacturing from scratch.
  • Teach everything. A technology that only one person knows is a technology one accident away from being lost.
  • Print the guides. Paper survives. Power grids don't. The printable packs are in the header.
