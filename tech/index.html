Rules of the tree

  • You can't skip the ladder. Each guide lists its prerequisites. A steam engine without iron is a rumor.
  • Scavenge before you build. Every guide lists what the ruins already provide.
  • Teach everything. A technology only one person knows is one accident from being lost.
  • Print the guides. Paper survives. Grids don't. See the print packs.

Mission

The Civilizational Recovery Project preserves humanity's operational knowledge — the instructions for making everything again — in an offline-first, print-ready archive. It is not a website. It is a backup of civilization.
