Pottery & Ceramic Vessels

Pottery & Ceramic Vessels

Pottery is the first chemistry. Firing clay transforms mud into stone — a permanent, sterilizable, liquid-tight container. Before pottery there is no boiling, no stored grain, no fermented drink, and later, no crucibles for metal.

Finding and preparing clay

  • Test: wet soil that can be rolled into a snake and bent without cracking has clay content. Rub some on your teeth — gritty is silt, smooth is clay.
  • Clean: dry it, crush it, and remove pebbles and organic matter.
  • Add temper: mix in sand, crushed fired pottery (grog), or crushed shell to stop the pot cracking during drying and firing.

Building methods (no wheel needed)

  • Coil method: roll clay into snakes, stack them in a spiral, and smooth the seams inside and out. Most reliable at A0.
  • Pinch pots: for small bowls and cups — press a ball of clay open with your thumbs.
  • Mold over a basket: clay spread over a basket form, fired to burn the basket away — a fast route to large vessels.

The drying trap

Clay shrinks as it dries. Air-dry slowly, in shade, for days — a pot dried too fast splits. Turn it regularly. It’s ready to fire only when completely dry (feels cool against your cheek = still wet inside).

Firing

  • Open fire firing: surround the dry pot with wood, build a careful teepee, and let the fire rise slowly over 1–2 hours. Fast temperature shock = cracked pots.
  • Target temperature: 600–800°C for basic pottery (red-hot) — enough for cooking pots.
  • Let it cool slowly, again. Thermal shock is the #1 killer.

Waterproofing

Fired earthenware is porous. Seal with:

  • Burnishing (polish while leather-hard) — the classic low-tech seal.
  • A thin slip wash before firing.
  • Beeswax rubbed into the warm pot.

What this unlocks

Boiling (a hot liquid-safe vessel), grain storage, fermentation crocks, soap making, and eventually crucibles and furnace linings for A2 metallurgy.

Validation checklist

  • Can you dig, clean, and temper local clay?
  • Can you produce a pot that holds water for 24 hours?
  • Can you fire it without cracking at least 3 times out of 4?
  • Is it waterproof enough to boil water?
