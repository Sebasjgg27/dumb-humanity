Water Wheel & Mechanical Power

The water wheel is humanity’s first engine that isn’t muscle. A river turns a wheel, the wheel turns an axle, gears convert the spin into grinding, sawing, pumping, and later — driving generators. It’s the bridge between animal strength and the steam engine, and it keeps working for centuries with minimal maintenance.

Types

TypeHow it worksBest use
UndershotFlat paddles hit by flowing water belowLow head, fast streams
OvershotWater pours into buckets on top, gravity turns wheelMost efficient (up to 85%), needs a head of water
BreastshotWater hits mid-heightMiddle ground
TurbineEnclosed, water jets through bladesA3+ high efficiency

Overshot is the A2 workhorse — build a millrace to deliver water to the top.

From rotation to work

  • Gears: wooden then iron gears (see lathe for the teeth) convert the slow, strong wheel turn into fast spindle spins (grinding) or slow heavy pulls (hammers, pumps).
  • Belt drives: leather/cloth belts transmit power across a workshop — one water wheel can run many machines (saw, hammer, grinder, pump).
  • The line shaft: a long axle runs through the workshop, machines belt off it. This is the “factory” pattern — centralized power, distributed work.

What it drives at A2

  • Grain mills — grinding flour without muscle (a civilization diet booster).
  • Trip hammers — forging iron and steel (see steel).
  • Pumps — dewatering mines.
  • Paper and textile machinery — paper, cloth.
  • Generators — the electricity node.

Siting and building

  • Millrace (a channel) with a controllable sluice gate for the water head.
  • The wheel’s axle runs on bearings; keep them greased (tallow/lanolin).
  • Winter: ice jams the wheel — build a shelter and a warm-water bypass.
  • A failed dam upstream is a flood — inspect upstream regularly.

Validation checklist

  • Can you build a water wheel that delivers usable shaft power?
  • Can you convert its rotation to grinding or hammering?
  • Can you drive multiple machines from one wheel?
  • Does it operate through a full season without major failure?
