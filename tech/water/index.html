Water: Acquisition & Purification

Water: Acquisition & Purification

Clean water is the single highest-priority survival task. Dehydration kills in days, unsafe water kills in weeks. Everything else on the ladder assumes you have a reliable, safe water supply.

The 3 rules of water

  1. Never drink unknown water untreated when you can avoid it.
  2. Boiling is the gold standard – 1 minute at a full rolling boil kills all pathogens (2–3 minutes at high altitude).
  3. The order matters: clarify → filter → boil → store.

Building a sand filter

A layered sand/charcoal filter removes particulates and many pathogens, but it does not replace boiling – it makes boiling more effective and the water taste better.

Layers from top to bottom in a container with a hole in the bottom:

  1. Coarse gravel (catches large debris)
  2. Fine gravel
  3. Coarse sand
  4. Fine sand
  5. Crushed charcoal (absorbs odors and some chemicals)
  6. Cloth as the final liner

Filter twice for heavy loads. Discard the first liter after building – it carries loose fines.

Solar disinfection (SODIS)

Clear plastic or glass bottles filled with clear water, laid in full sun for 6+ hours (2 days if cloudy). UV kills most pathogens. Works, but is slower and less reliable than boiling. Never use for cold, cloudy days as sole treatment.

Boiling the right way

  • Boil, don’t just heat. A rolling boil for 1 minute.
  • Let it cool before storing in a clean, sealed container.
  • Keep it covered – recontamination is the most common failure.

Emergency bleach dosing (only for clear water)

  • 5% household bleach, no additives: 2 drops per liter (1 teaspoon per 5 gallons).
  • Stir, wait 30 minutes before drinking.
  • Smell must be faintly of chlorine; if not, re-dose once.

Signs of trouble

Diarrhea within days of a new source = stop using it. Assume all surface water is contaminated unless you personally tested it.

Validation checklist

  • Can you produce 2 liters of safe water per person per day?
  • Do you have a method that works without electricity?
  • Is your storage container clean and sealed?
  • Does your group know which sources are reliable?
