The framework

The Rebuild Ladder

Inspired by the InfoPreserver project's capability ladder and Dr. Stone's staged tech progression. Each level answers one question. You cannot skip rungs — but every rung mastered makes the next one faster.

A0

Days – Weeks

Survival Baseline

Can humans stay alive?

1 of 6

Water, fire, shelter, first aid, and the first hand-made tools. The absolute floor for keeping a group alive.

Dr. Stone parallel: Senku’s first three months: stone tools, rope, bow drill, fire.
A1

Months – Years

Stabilized Community

Can people live here sustainably?

2 of 6

Agriculture, food preservation, permanent shelter, sanitation, soap, and the social structures that let a village persist across seasons and generations.

Dr. Stone parallel: Ishigami Village: farming, pottery, weaving, a settled way of life.
A2

Years – Decades

Industrial Seed

Can we bootstrap industry?

3 of 6

The non-linear acceleration point. Charcoal, iron, chemicals, the lathe, steam, and electricity unlock everything after them. The hardest and most valuable stage.

Dr. Stone parallel: The Kingdom of Science: blast furnace, sulfuric acid, generators, radio.
A3

Decades

Infrastructure Recovery

Can systems scale beyond one town?

4 of 6

Regional waterworks, electrical grids, rail networks, cold chains, and public health systems that bind many communities together.

Dr. Stone parallel: The Perseus, GPS, hydroelectric dams, paved roads.
A4

Generations

Advanced Industry & Science

Precision, optimization, research

5 of 6

Transistor electronics, integrated circuits, modern chemistry and pharmaceuticals, and the return of scientific research as an institution.

Dr. Stone parallel: The silicon road, vacuum tubes, the parametron computer.
A5

Long Horizon

Frontier & Leapfrogs

High-dependency systems for future societies

6 of 6

Aerospace, satellites, and advanced biotechnology. Only worth building after the ladder below is solid. Also: ways to leapfrog the whole ladder.

Dr. Stone parallel: The moon mission, contact with Why-Man.
