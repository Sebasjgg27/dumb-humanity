Inicio / Disasters / Hurricane / Typhoon
Natural Danger: Grave (4/5)

Hurricane / Typhoon

1 Priority Actions

  1. 1 Evacuate if ordered — do not delay or try to ride it out
  2. 2 Board up windows with plywood or tape windows in an X pattern
  3. 3 Fill bathtubs and containers with water before the storm hits
  4. 4 Move to an interior room on the lowest floor if sheltering in place
  5. 5 Stay away from windows — flying glass is a leading cause of injury

Key Dangers

  • Wind speeds exceeding 250 km/h in Category 5 storms
  • Storm surge — ocean water pushed inland, often the deadliest threat
  • Tornadoes spawned by hurricane bands
  • Widespread flooding from 200-500mm of rain
  • Extended power outages lasting days to weeks

Shelter

Interior room on lowest floor — bathroom, closet, or hallway. Away from all windows. Concrete or brick buildings are safest. Mobile homes are death traps — evacuate to a shelter.

Water

Fill all containers before the storm. Water heaters hold 30-80 gallons of clean water. Do not drink floodwater. After the storm, boil or purify all water — sewage systems will be compromised.

Fire

Minimal fire risk during the storm — too wet. After the storm, watch for downed power lines causing sparks. Use generators OUTSIDE only — carbon monoxide kills.

Food

Canned goods and non-perishables stockpiled before the storm. Open the refrigerator only when necessary — food spoils quickly without power. Eat perishable items first.

Signals & Rescue

  • Bright fabric on roof after the storm
  • Whistle if trapped in debris
  • Mirror flash toward search aircraft
  • Stay visible in open areas for helicopter rescue
  • Mark your location with large visible signs

Common Mistakes

  • Opening windows to equalize pressure — myth, it causes damage
  • Going outside during the eye of the storm — the other side is coming
  • Driving through floodwater — 30cm of water can sweep away a car
  • Using candles during power outage — fire risk with structural damage
  • Ignoring evacuation orders to protect property
