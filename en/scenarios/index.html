Universal Survival Rules

STOP

Sit, Think, Observe, Plan. Panic kills more people than the emergency itself.

Signal Early

Make yourself findable before doing anything else. Rescue is always the fastest way to safety.

Rule of 3s

3 hours without shelter, 3 days without water, 3 weeks without food. Prioritize accordingly.

Mission

Dumb Humanity preserves humanity's operational knowledge — the instructions for making everything again — in an offline-first, print-ready archive. It is not a website. It is a backup of civilization.
