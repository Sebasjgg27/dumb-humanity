Blizzard / Snowstorm

1 Priority Actions

  1. 1 Stay indoors — do not attempt to travel during the blizzard
  2. 2 Conserve heat: close off unused rooms, stuff towels under doors
  3. 3 If stranded in a vehicle, stay inside and run engine 10 minutes per hour for heat
  4. 4 Check on neighbors, especially elderly or those with medical needs
  5. 5 Use alternative heating safely — avoid indoor open flames

Key Dangers

  • Hypothermia — core temperature drops below 35°C and is fatal
  • Carbon monoxide poisoning from improper indoor heating
  • Snow accumulation collapsing roofs — especially flat roofs
  • Power outages lasting days in severe storms
  • Whiteout conditions making travel impossible and disorienting

Shelter

Stay in your home. Close off unused rooms to conserve heat. Layer clothing and use blankets. If pipes may freeze, let faucets drip. Move to an interior room at night for warmth.

Water

Pipes may freeze — melt snow on a stove or in a sealed container. Do NOT eat snow directly — it lowers body temperature. Keep bottled water accessible before the storm. Hot water from water heater is safe.

Fire

Use fireplace or wood stove if available — ensure chimney is clear. NEVER use a gas oven or outdoor grill for indoor heating — carbon monoxide kills silently. Space heaters need 1 meter clearance from everything.

Food

Stockpile non-perishable food before the storm. Canned goods, dried food, peanut butter, crackers. Eat perishable items first before they spoil. Ration carefully if storm extends beyond expectations.

Signals & Rescue

  • Bright fabric visible against white snow
  • If stranded on road, tie bright cloth to antenna
  • Mirror flash is extremely visible against snow
  • Three fires in triangle pattern
  • Sound: banging on metal pipes or vehicle horn

Common Mistakes

  • Going outside without full protection — frostbite in minutes
  • Using outdoor heating devices indoors — carbon monoxide poisoning
  • Driving during the storm — most blizzard deaths happen in vehicles
  • Ignoring hypothermia signs — confusion and drowsiness precede death
  • Running the car engine with exhaust pipe blocked by snow
