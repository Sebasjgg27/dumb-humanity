Design principles

Built for permanence

  • Offline-first

    Every article is static HTML. Download the ZIP and the entire library works without power, network, or servers.

  • Print is permanent

    Power grids fail. Screens die. Paper outlives both. Every guide is print-optimized with printable chapter packs.

  • Validated & reproducible

    Every procedure lists materials, tools, steps, and the science behind it — reproducible with progressively available technology.

Mission

Dumb Humanity preserves humanity's operational knowledge — the instructions for making everything again — in an offline-first, print-ready archive. It is not a website. It is a backup of civilization.
