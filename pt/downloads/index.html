Doutrina de redundância

  • Three copies, two media, one far away. Keep a digital copy, a printed copy, and a sealed backup stored separately.
  • Print masters, then copy. Keep one master set dry and sealed; make working copies.
  • Copy the ZIP to multiple devices — a phone, a laptop, a USB drive, an old e-reader. Devices fail; copies survive.
  • Teach it. The most durable format is a person who can explain the knowledge to the next generation.
