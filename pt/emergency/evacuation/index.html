SAIR RÁPIDO

Planos de Evacuação

Quando sair, onde ir e o que levar. Rapidez salva vidas.

Wildfire Evacuation

Leave 2-4 hours before fire arrival

Wildfire approaching your area

Rota

  1. 1 Monitor fire progress — know 2 escape routes from your area
  2. 2 Pack evacuation bag (documents, water, first aid, cash, medications)
  3. 3 Move vehicles facing outward, load essential supplies
  4. 4 Close all windows and doors, remove flammable items from around house
  5. 5 Leave early — do not wait to see the fire
  6. 6 Follow designated evacuation routes, avoid shortcuts through vegetation

Lista de verificação

  • Go-bag packed and by door
  • Vehicles fueled and ready
  • Important documents in waterproof bag
  • Medications and glasses
  • Water and food for 3 days
  • Phone charger and battery pack
  • Cash in small bills
  • Pet supplies if applicable

Flood Evacuation

Evacuate immediately when warned — minutes count in flash floods

Rising water levels, flash flood warning

Rota

  1. 1 Move to higher ground immediately — do not wait for orders
  2. 2 Never walk or drive through moving water — 6 inches can knock you down
  3. 3 Avoid bridges over fast-moving water
  4. 4 If trapped in a building, go to the highest floor — do not go to a closed attic
  5. 5 If water is rising inside, get on the roof and signal for help

Lista de verificação

  • Go-bag with waterproof container
  • Waterproof bag for electronics
  • Medications
  • Change of warm clothes in waterproof bag
  • Flashlight and batteries
  • Whistle for signaling

Earthquake Evacuation

Evacuate immediately after shaking stops if building is damaged

Major earthquake — aftershocks and structural damage expected

Rota

  1. 1 During shaking: Drop, Cover, Hold On — under sturdy furniture
  2. 2 After shaking stops: check for injuries, put out small fires
  3. 3 Evacuate building if damaged — use stairs, never elevators
  4. 4 Move to open area away from buildings, power lines, and trees
  5. 5 Expect aftershocks — be ready to drop and cover again
  6. 6 If trapped: tap on pipes or walls, do not shout — conserve energy

Lista de verificação

  • Sturdy shoes by bed
  • Flashlight within reach
  • Emergency whistle
  • First aid kit
  • Go-bag near exit
  • Know safe spots in each room

Hurricane Evacuation

Evacuate 24-48 hours before expected landfall

Hurricane or typhoon approaching — mandatory evacuation zone

Rota

  1. 1 Evacuate when ordered — do not wait for the storm to arrive
  2. 2 Follow designated evacuation routes — expect heavy traffic
  3. 3 Fill vehicles with fuel before leaving
  4. 4 Secure or board up windows with plywood
  5. 5 Move important items to highest interior room if not evacuating
  6. 6 Turn off utilities at main switches if time permits

Lista de verificação

  • Go-bag with 5 days of supplies
  • Cash (ATMs may be down)
  • Full fuel tank
  • Important documents
  • Medications for 2 weeks
  • Pet carriers and food
  • Maps of evacuation routes
  • Battery-powered weather radio

Nuclear Event Evacuation

Act within minutes — radiation exposure decreases rapidly with distance and shielding

Nuclear explosion, reactor incident, or fallout warning

Rota

  1. 1 If indoors: stay inside — building provides shielding from fallout
  2. 2 If outdoors: find the nearest sturdy building immediately
  3. 3 Move to the center of the building, away from windows and roof
  4. 4 Remove outer clothing and seal in a plastic bag
  5. 5 Shower or wash exposed skin thoroughly
  6. 6 Listen for official instructions on evacuation or shelter-in-place

Lista de verificação

  • Potassium iodide tablets if in affected region
  • Dust masks or cloth for breathing
  • Sealed water supply
  • Canned food with manual opener
  • Plastic sheeting and duct tape for sealing room
  • Battery-powered radio
  • Geiger counter if available
