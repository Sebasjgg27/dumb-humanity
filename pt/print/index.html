Dicas de impressão

  • Paper quality: acid-free bond paper lasts decades; ordinary copier paper lasts years.
  • Ink: laser printer toner is far more waterproof than inkjet.
  • Lamination: for the pages you'll use outdoors — water, fire, first aid.
  • Reproduction: a photocopier is itself a technology worth preserving. Print masters, then copy.
  • Distribution: keep one master set sealed and dry; make working copies.

Missão

Dumb Humanity preserves humanity's operational knowledge — the instructions for making everything again — in an offline-first, print-ready archive. It is not a website. It is a backup of civilization.
